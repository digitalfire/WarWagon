# Research and evidence standards

## Evidence levels
- E0 hypothesis, intuition or unverified claim
- E1 documented project claim with dated link and revision
- E2 code inspection or reproducible implementation test
- E3 controlled comparative benchmark with consistent conditions
- E4 repeated independent reproduction

Never silently upgrade evidence level. GitHub popularity, launch claims, vendor benchmarks and news use are not equivalent to comparative performance.

## Research dossier
Every dossier records: research question, alternatives, versioned source URLs, citations, observed evidence, conflicting findings, validity threats, benchmark protocol, reproduction commands, license considerations, open questions and decision impact.

## Controlled comparisons
Normalize model, prompt budget, tool availability, target fixture, time window, scope, hardware and ground-truth scoring. Repeat randomized trials, report median and dispersion, distinguish false positives from verified findings, and archive raw trajectories. Explicitly account for cost, success and side effects.

## Decision discipline
Document risk of premature optimization; prefer reversible adapters and contracts where evidence is weak. A high-capability research direction may remain an experimental track without becoming a production dependency.
