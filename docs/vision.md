# WarWagon vision

Independent, open-source, capability-first research into persistent collaborative agent intelligence for authorized security assessments.

**North star:** specialized agents share grounded knowledge, explore divergent hypotheses, compete across planning strategies, and produce reproducible evidence while surviving long-running interruptions.

## Priorities
1. Parallel graph-grounded exploration and open-ended hypothesis generation.
2. Adaptable hierarchical, fact-intent, and hybrid planners.
3. Durable autonomous multi-agent execution.
4. Working, episodic, semantic, procedural, and experimental memory.
5. Provider-agnostic local and external model inference.
6. Replaceable typed adapters for security tools and external frameworks.
7. Independent evidence verification.
8. Reproducible scientific evaluation and performance improvement.

**Capability first** means avoiding unnecessary architectural constraints on research. Actual interactions with target systems remain governed by authorized scope and isolated execution; those controls are a discrete subsystem rather than the core research focus.

Immediate milestone: prove multi-agent coordination, shared knowledge, recovery, and verified evidence in a controlled fixture. Not a production-ready platform or polished UI.
